About us

GoodFood started with a simple challenge: eating healthy can feel expensive and complicated, especially for students.

Our goal is to make budget-friendly ingredients easier to find and help people turn them into meals that are quick, nutritious and affordable.

Student prototype for BSAD 281 at St. Francis Xavier University. Not a real company — all data is fictional.
